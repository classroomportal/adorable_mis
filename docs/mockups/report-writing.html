End of Term 1 Reports 2026/27 · Years 7–12

Sample data: made-up students, not real records

Where the numbers come from

  • Grades this year: every row in results for that student and subject since the start of the academic year, short tests and the term exam, in date order.
  • Target: target_grades. Each grade is coloured against it using the points in grade_scale (A* = 8 … G = 1).
  • Behaviour: behaviour_events since 1 September, split into positive and negative points with the categories behind them.
  • Effort, presentation and homework grades live on report_subject_comments. Effort is already there; presentation and homework would be two new columns on the same scale. The mentor screen shows whatever subject teachers have entered so far.
  • The AI draft is only given the facts listed under “What the AI draft is based on”. It is told not to invent anything else, as the current draft button already is.
